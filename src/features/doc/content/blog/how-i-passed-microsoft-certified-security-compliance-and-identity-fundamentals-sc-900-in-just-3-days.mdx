Last week, I passed the Microsoft Certified: Security, Compliance, and Identity Fundamentals (SC-900) exam after studying for only 3 days.

This certification focuses on Microsoft’s approach to security, compliance, and identity, and how tools like Microsoft Entra, Defender, and Purview work together to protect organizations in the cloud.

## Why I took the exam

After passing Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900), I wanted to continue learning how Microsoft handles security in the cloud. The SC-900 felt like the natural next step. I was already familiar with basic cloud concepts, so this one felt easier and more focused.

## My 3-day study plan

I kept my preparation simple and direct:

- **Day 1–2**: Studied Microsoft Learn’s SC-900 modules to understand key topics and structure.
- **Day 3**: Watched Pete Zerger’s _SC-900 Exam Cram_ on YouTube for quick review, and took one practice test from Andrew Brown (ExamPro) to test my readiness.

The next day, I booked the exam and passed on the first try.

## My thoughts on the exam

Compared to AZ-900, this one was definitely easier. The questions were more straightforward, and since I already had a foundation in Azure, most of the topics felt familiar. It focused mainly on understanding concepts, not deep technical details.

## Key lessons

- Use Microsoft Learn as your main resource — it’s free and straight from Microsoft.
- Pete Zerger’s video gives you a clear, fast overview before the exam.
- Don’t overcomplicate your prep. You only need 2–3 solid resources and consistency.

The SC-900 is a great certification if you’re starting in cybersecurity or cloud security. It builds a strong foundation and connects well to more advanced Microsoft security certifications.

Passing it in just 3 days reminded me that sometimes, keeping things simple and focused works best.
