AWS is offering 100% free certification vouchers for its top exams in 2025! This is a great opportunity to earn AWS certifications in areas like Cloud Computing, Solutions Architecture, Machine Learning, and more — at no cost. If you’re looking to enhance your skills and improve your career prospects, now is the perfect time to get started. Here’s a detailed breakdown of how you can claim your voucher and get ahead in the cloud industry.

## How to claim your free AWS certification voucher: a step-by-step guide

AWS is offering vouchers for two levels of certifications: Foundational and Associate. You can earn vouchers for certifications such as:

- **Foundational vouchers**: AWS Cloud Practitioner, AWS AI Practitioner.
- **Associate vouchers**: Solutions Architect, Developer, SysOps Administrator, Data Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer.

These certifications are some of the most recognized and sought-after qualifications in the tech industry, especially in the field of cloud computing.

### Step 1: create accounts on AWS Educate & AWS Skill Builder

The first thing you need to do is create accounts on two platforms:

1. **AWS Educate**: This platform provides free resources for learning about AWS. You’ll need this to complete some of the required courses.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Be sure to use your school or work email when signing up, not a personal Gmail address. This ensures you’re eligible for the free vouchers.

### Step 2: complete a short AWS Educate course

Next, head over to AWS Educate and complete a short introductory course. A good starting point is the _Introduction to Generative AI_ course, which takes about 30 minutes. This course will introduce you to AWS and its cloud services, while also earning you points toward your voucher.

### Step 3: join the Emerging Talent Community (ETC)

Now that you’re set up, it’s time to join the Emerging Talent Community (ETC). By joining this community, you’ll earn points by completing surveys and staying engaged with the content.

<FramedImage
  src="/blogs/inside-AWS-FREE-Certification5.webp"
  alt="AWS Emerging Talent Community home page"
/>

- **Complete surveys**: You’ll earn 2,000 points just for completing the initial surveys.
- **Earn weekly points**: Stay active in the community and earn 360 points every week for continued engagement.

This step is key to accumulating the points needed to claim your voucher, so be sure to stay involved regularly.

### Step 4: claim your voucher

Once you’ve earned enough points, head to the ETC Rewards page. Here, you can redeem your points for an AWS certification voucher. Make sure to complete the required courses and pass the practice exam with a 70% or higher score, as this is necessary to qualify for the voucher.

<FramedImage
  src="/blogs/inside-AWS-FREE-Certification6.webp"
  alt="AWS Emerging Talent Community rewards page"
/>

#### Voucher requirements

- **Foundational vouchers** (Cloud Practitioner, AI Practitioner):
  - Earn 4,500 points.
  - Complete the prep course and pass the practice exam with 70% or higher.
- **Associate vouchers** (Solutions Architect, Developer, SysOps, etc.):
  - Earn 5,200 points.
  - Complete the prep course and pass the practice exam with 70% or higher.

Once you’ve completed all the steps, your voucher will be sent directly to your email!
