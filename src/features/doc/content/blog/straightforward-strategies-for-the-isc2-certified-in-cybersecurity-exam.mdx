In cybersecurity, a certification can help you stand out. My experience with the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) certification was challenging but rewarding. Here’s how I prepared, what the exam was like, and tips to help you succeed.

## Building a strong foundation: preparation strategy

- **Start with free resources**: I began with the free Certified in Cybersecurity Specialization on Coursera. It provided a solid foundation in essential concepts.
- **Enroll in courses**: I took _The Ultimate ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) Course ’24_ on Udemy, which included training and practice exams.
- **Use practice tests**: I completed practice tests from LinkedIn Learning and Coursera to check my understanding.
- **Access additional materials**: I found helpful resources on GitHub that outlined exam objectives and included extra study materials.

## The power of consistency: study routine

I studied for one hour each day for 14 days, breaking down topics into smaller sections. This approach helped me reinforce my knowledge and build confidence before the exam.

## Conquering the exam: experience on test day

On exam day, I felt a mix of excitement and nerves. The questions were more challenging than I expected and tested my knowledge in new ways. When I got my results and heard, “Congratulations!” I felt relieved — I had passed!

## Strategies for success: tips to ace the exam

- **Practice tests**: Take as many practice tests as you can to get familiar with the exam format and question types.
- **Know your objectives**: Understand each exam objective and use related resources for your studies.
- **Stay committed**: Maintain a consistent study routine to reinforce your knowledge and boost your confidence.

## Next steps

The ISC2 CC exam costs $50 for the first year to obtain your certification and digital badge. Keep in mind that this certification expires in three years, so plan for renewal.

This certification experience was transformative. With dedication and the right strategies, you can find new opportunities in cybersecurity. Good luck!
